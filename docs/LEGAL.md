# Legal & Disclaimers

**AI Travel Platform is an informational / advisory tool. It is not a travel
agency, booking engine, or payment processor.** It does not sell tickets, take
payments, hold reservations, or provide visa/legal advice.

## Prices are indicative
All prices shown are **indicative estimates**, aggregated from public sources or
derived from a distance/star model, and labeled with their source and the time
they were fetched ("as of"). They **may be out of date or inaccurate** and are
**not an offer**. Always confirm the final price on the provider's own site
before making any decision. Estimated (non-fetched) figures are marked
`~ indicative` in the UI.

## Data sources & scraping
The platform can aggregate prices from public web sources. Where it fetches
third-party pages:

- It identifies with a normal browser User-Agent and respects anti-bot
  responses by **skipping** a blocked source — it does **not** solve CAPTCHAs,
  rotate identities, or otherwise circumvent access controls.
- Some sources' Terms of Service restrict automated access. **Enabling and
  operating specific sources is the operator's responsibility**, and the
  operator accepts the associated ToS/legal risk. No source is contacted for
  commercial resale of its data; results are shown for the user's own
  informational use with attribution (`source`, `fetched_at`).
- FX rates (Frankfurter/ECB), map tiles (OpenStreetMap, ODbL — attribution
  required) and places/POI data (OpenStreetMap, Travelpayouts public endpoints)
  are open/free sources used under their respective terms.

## AI-generated content
Itineraries and chat responses are generated by a language model and may contain
errors. The model is constrained so that **every price it reports must appear in
the fetched source text** (a verbatim guardrail); it does not invent prices.
Narrative suggestions are still advisory and should be verified.

## Privacy
The platform collects **no personal data for commercial use**. Optional usage
analytics are **aggregate and privacy-safe** (counts of anonymous events such as
"plan computed"); they contain no personal identifiers, no trip contents, and no
tracking across sites. See `docs/OPS.md` for configuration.
